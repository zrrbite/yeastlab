# yeastlab

A working notebook for everything yeast — sourdough, baker's yeast bread, and beer. Built around the idea that fermentation is one craft with many costumes: the same enzymes (amylase, protease) and the same microbes (yeast, lactic acid bacteria) drive bread, beer, and almost every other slow-fermented food. Understand the chemistry once, then stage time and temperature for whatever you're making.

## Where to start

- **[`sourdough/methods/double-enzymatic-activation.md`](sourdough/methods/double-enzymatic-activation.md)** — the headline method. A low-maintenance cold-fed-levain workflow built around enzyme timing rather than recipe slavishness. Start here if you want to bake.
- **[`theory/`](theory/)** — the science underneath. Fermentation pathways, enzyme behavior, flavor chemistry, water minerals, and how brewing and baking borrow from each other.
- **[`sourdough/`](sourdough/)** — wild yeast + LAB: starter maintenance, flour selection, recipes.
- **[`bakers-yeast/`](bakers-yeast/)** — commercial *S. cerevisiae* breads (pizza, pita, rolls).
- **[`beer/`](beer/)** — brewers yeast: ales, lagers, mixed cultures, recipes.

## Philosophy

> Think like a builder, not a follower. Your formula is a starting ratio, not a rulebook.

These docs explain *why* a step works, not just *what* to do. Once you know how the enzymes and microbes behave, the recipes write themselves and the schedule bends to your life instead of the other way around.
