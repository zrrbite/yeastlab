# Theory

Cross-cutting science. Most of what's in here applies equally to bread, beer, and coffee — the same enzymes, the same microbes, the same chemistry, just staged differently.

## Core chemistry

- **[`fermentation.md`](fermentation.md)** — what fermentation actually is, the two players (yeast + LAB), the chemistry inside the cell, the joint sourdough equation.
- **[`enzymes.md`](enzymes.md)** — amylase and protease, the autolytic process, gluten formation, the amylase paradox, hydration ↔ enzyme activity.
- **[`flavor-chemistry.md`](flavor-chemistry.md)** — where flavor comes from: acids, alcohols, esters, diacetyl, Maillard. Tuning levers for sweet vs tangy vs buttery vs fruity. The bread Maillard flavor map.
- **[`water-minerals.md`](water-minerals.md)** — Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, HCO₃⁻ and friends — what each ion does to fermentation and gluten.

## Cross-craft references

- **[`yeast-strains.md`](yeast-strains.md)** — every yeast (and LAB, and Brett) used across crafts, organized by organism. The same *S. cerevisiae* in your bread, your beer, and your wine.
- **[`fermentation-temperature.md`](fermentation-temperature.md)** — temperature playbook across all crafts. What every temperature does to esters, acids, enzymes, and final character.
- **[`bread-vs-beer.md`](bread-vs-beer.md)** — same enzymes, same tricks. Side-by-side process map and ideas that cross over.

## Reading order

For someone new to the cross-craft view:

1. **[`fermentation.md`](fermentation.md)** — start here. What fermentation IS.
2. **[`enzymes.md`](enzymes.md)** — what enzymes do alongside fermentation.
3. **[`flavor-chemistry.md`](flavor-chemistry.md)** — where flavor comes from. The most useful single file for recipe-design intuition.
4. **[`yeast-strains.md`](yeast-strains.md)** — the cast of characters across all crafts.
5. **[`fermentation-temperature.md`](fermentation-temperature.md)** — what temperature does, everywhere.
6. **[`water-minerals.md`](water-minerals.md)** — water chemistry, applies to all brewing.
7. **[`bread-vs-beer.md`](bread-vs-beer.md)** — bridge between specific crafts.

Read these once and the recipes stop feeling like rules and start feeling like dials.
