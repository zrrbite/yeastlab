# Theory

Cross-cutting science. Most of what's in here applies equally to bread and beer — the same enzymes, the same microbes, the same chemistry, just staged differently.

- **[`fermentation.md`](fermentation.md)** — what fermentation actually is, the two players (yeast + LAB), the chemistry inside the cell, the joint sourdough equation.
- **[`enzymes.md`](enzymes.md)** — amylase and protease, the autolytic process, gluten formation, the amylase paradox, hydration ↔ enzyme activity.
- **[`flavor-chemistry.md`](flavor-chemistry.md)** — where flavor comes from: acids, alcohols, esters, diacetyl, Maillard. Tuning levers for sweet vs tangy vs buttery vs fruity. The bread Maillard flavor map.
- **[`water-minerals.md`](water-minerals.md)** — Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, HCO₃⁻ and friends — what each ion does to fermentation and gluten.
- **[`bread-vs-beer.md`](bread-vs-beer.md)** — same enzymes, same tricks. Side-by-side process map and ideas that cross over.

Read these once and the recipes stop feeling like rules and start feeling like dials.
